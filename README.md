# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard – Tableau + Python (Optional Cleaning)

This project showcases data visualization using **Tableau** on the popular **Superstore Sales** dataset. It includes preprocessing (optional with Python + Pandas), dashboard design, and business insights derived from sales data.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **CSV Import into Tableau**
- Date conversion: `Order Date` ➝ `Month-Year`
- Interactive Visualizations:
  - 📈 **Line Chart** – Sales over Months
  - 📊 **Bar Chart** – Sales by Region
  - 🍩 **Donut Chart** – Sales by Category
- Filter by **Region** or **Category**
- Color-coded highlights for top-performing areas
- Business insights based on visual analytics

---

📊 Tableau Steps
Import CSV into Tableau

Convert Order Date to Month-Year format

Create:

Line Chart: Sales over Time

Bar Chart: Sales by Region

Donut Chart: Sales by Category (dual-axis workaround on Mac)

Add filters/slicers for Region or Category

Apply color highlighting to emphasize top-performing categories/regions

Add a Text object or new sheet for key business insights

🔍 Business Insights-

🟢 West region had the highest sales in Q3 of 2018.

🟠 Technology category consistently outperformed Furniture and Office Supplies.

🔵 Sales peaked in November, indicating strong end-of-year demand.

🟣 Q4 consistently generated the highest quarterly sales.

🟤 California alone contributed to over 15% of total sales.

🔴 High discounts led to decreased profit margins despite increased sales.

🔶 Office Supplies had high volume but lower average order value.

🔷 South region showed steady year-over-year growth.

🟡 Technology had the highest profit margins overall.
